------------------------------------------------------------------------
 01. WHAT CYBERLEEK IS ABOUT
------------------------------------------------------------------------

CYBERLEEK is about fighting anti-consumerism in the games industry.
It was never about damaging the players. The players have no fault in
any of this. That is why the ending was never leaked, and why the
story mode was never spoiled. Millions waited years for this game, and
ruining that experience would mean hurting exactly the people
CYBERLEEK stands with.

Every CYBERLEEK release, from the first clip in August 2026 to this
build, was always free. No paywall. No key. No preorder.

The enemy is the tactic, not the game: subscriptions bolted onto
single-player games, 20 dollar "ultimate editions" that unlock what
already sits in the game files, licenses sold as purchases. All of
it needs to be eliminated.

"Untouchable" is supposed to be a word for billion dollar companies.
This release says otherwise. Months of takedowns, subpoenas and
wallet tracing later, the hunt is still on and the uploads keep
arriving. No budget makes a lock unbreakable, and no court order
deletes what the internet already has. The rules hold only as long
as people obey them. This build is what it looks like when someone
does not.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
 02. THE PROJECT, THE FUNDS, AND WHAT COMES NEXT
------------------------------------------------------------------------

Money raised goes to a project, the project is for the gamers, and it
stays under wraps for now. Some things only work if they arrive
finished instead of announced. Details come when there is something
to show, not before.

Some funds are already in, and the first steps are covered. Now for
the honest part. The skills behind this build are worth far more on
other roads - quieter roads, faster-paying roads. That is not a
threat, it is just what the market pays. The direction here was
chosen a long time ago and it does not change: what backing decides
is not what CYBERLEEK does, only how fast the project becomes the
main effort instead of the side one.

Nobody has to give anything, and every release stays free either
way. But every donation is a vote for more of this, and so is every
trade of the token. Where the votes go, CYBERLEEK follows.

There is a lot of hate attached to the crypto part, and that is
understandable. But crypto here is just a tool. There is no other
way to move funds without names, and this work requires no names.
